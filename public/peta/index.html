    Methodology and sources

    • The unit is the province (38). Correlations between provinces are not causal and say nothing about individual voters.
    • The MBG program began in January 2025, after the 2024 election.
    • SPPG counts follow population size, so a version per 100k people in poverty is included.
    • With n = 38, Spearman's ρ is a hint, not proof. Ties use average ranks; p-values use the t approximation (df = 36).
    • Margin = winner's share minus runner-up's share, in percentage points.
    • Poverty, schooling and SPPG per 100k use quantiles (5 classes); bivariate uses terciles (3 × 3).
    • Ticket colors are only markers and imply no judgment.

    Sources: KPU, official 2024 presidential tally (domestic votes); BPS, Susenas March 2026 (poverty); BPS, HDI (mean years of schooling); National Nutrition Agency, SPPG locations as of 8 June 2026; boundaries: 38 Indonesian provinces (GeoJSON). Map by Ananda Gunawan, November 2026.
